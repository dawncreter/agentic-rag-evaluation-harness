# Proposal Review Standard (Northwind Aerospace — fictional)

## Review gates
Every proposal passes three review gates: Blue Team for strategy, Pink Team for the first full draft, and Red Team for the final draft. Gold Team gives executive approval before submission.

## Turnaround targets
Pink Team reviews are completed within 2 business days. Red Team reviews are completed within 3 business days. Reviewers record findings in the proposal tracker, not in email.

## Compliance matrix
Each proposal includes a compliance matrix that maps every requirement in the solicitation to a section of the response. The capture manager owns the compliance matrix.

## Use of AI drafting tools
AI drafting tools may only use the approved past-performance library. Every AI-generated paragraph must cite its source document, and a named author remains accountable for the final text.
