# Records Retention Policy (Northwind Aerospace — fictional)

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors and subcontractors who create or store company records. It covers email, engineering drawings, contracts and financial records.

## Retention periods
Contract records are retained for 7 years after contract close-out. Engineering drawings for flight hardware are retained for the life of the program plus 10 years. Routine email is deleted automatically after 3 years unless it is placed on legal hold.

## Legal holds
Legal may place any record on hold during litigation or an investigation. Records on legal hold must not be deleted until Legal releases the hold in writing.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Chief Compliance Officer. Approved exceptions are reviewed every 12 months.
